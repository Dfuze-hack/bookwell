    Our Mission

    Many students don't have access to good books or study materials. BookWell's goal is to make quality learning resources easy to find and available in the languages students speak. We prioritize clear licensing and review listings before publication.

    What We Do

    Curate

    We find free, openly licensed textbooks and study materials and organize them by class and subject.

    Check

    Every resource is reviewed for accuracy, language quality, and licensing before it's added.

    Share

    Resources are shared for free through this website and partner schools and community groups.

    About BookWell

    BookWell is a student-led, volunteer-run project. Every volunteer works remotely, and an adult coordinator reviews all content and safeguarding decisions. We're growing step by step, and we welcome feedback from teachers, students, and families.

    Funding

    BookWell is volunteer-run. We do not claim grants, sponsors, or financial support that have not been verified.

    Locations

    BookWell operates online, with volunteers contributing remotely.

    Creator Studio

    Upload and manage PDF books and other supported listings. Administrators can publish, unpublish, and archive any listing. Authorized creators can create drafts, upload their own private files and covers, and edit their own unpublished listings. Creators cannot change another creator's products, publish listings, or assign roles.

    Studio sign in

    Admin tools require a configured Supabase project. Administrator accounts must be provisioned separately; public sign-up is disabled.
